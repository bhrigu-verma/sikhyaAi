## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Music:** YouTube Audio Library → search "hopeful acoustic" or "inspiring instrumental".
Tracks to try: "Mountain" by Vendredi, "Aube" by Atch, or any soft piano + strings.

**Voiceover:** Record in a quiet room. Speak 10% slower than feels natural — it sounds right on video. One take for whole script ideally for consistent tone.

**Text style for overlays:**
- Font: Bold sans-serif (Helvetica/Inter)
- Color: White text, black background or transparent dark overlay
- Punjabi text: slightly larger, amber color (#F59E0B)
