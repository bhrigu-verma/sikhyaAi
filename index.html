✦ How Sikhya Works

Built as a complete learning system.

Not just a chatbot

Sikhya is a retrieval-augmented learning system built specifically around PSEB curriculum documents, past papers, and chapter-level knowledge.

When you ask a question, Sikhya doesn't just call a language model — it first retrieves the most relevant passages from your actual textbook, reranks them for accuracy, and then generates a syllabus-grounded answer.

Your uploaded photos go through an OCR pipeline before reaching the AI. Every answer includes a traceable source in your textbook.
