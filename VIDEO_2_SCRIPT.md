# Video 2 — School Visit Documentary
## "We Took It To a Real Punjab School"
### Target: 4–6 minutes | Authentic | Story-driven

---

## CONCEPT

This is the "proof" video. Video 1 said what Sikhya is.
This video shows it working in the real world, with real students who had nothing to do with building it.

Tone: Warm. Honest. Not over-produced. Let the students carry it.

---

## EDITING GUIDE FOR VIDEO 2

**Subtitles:** Add for all Punjabi/Hindi speech. White text, black outline. CapCut auto-generates these.

**Structure in edit:**
1. Don't cut too fast. Let silences and reactions breathe — 1–2 sec after a student finishes talking before cutting.
2. The moment the TTS speaks Punjabi and a student reacts — **do not cut this**. Let it play fully.
3. Use split audio: school ambience under your voiceover sections gives warmth.
4. Lower-thirds for student names (optional — ask permission first): "Manpreet, Class 10"

**Music:**
- Intro/setup: soft Punjabi folk-inspired instrumental (search "Punjab instrumental background" on YouTube Audio Library)
- Feedback section: no music or very low — let voices dominate
- Outro montage: same music as Video 1 for brand continuity

## SHOOT DAY CHECKLIST

**Before you go:**
- [ ] App working on mobile (test on phone data, not wifi — school may have bad wifi)
- [ ] Sikhya loaded and logged in on 2 devices
- [ ] Lapel mic charged/clipped
- [ ] Phone storage > 10GB free
- [ ] Permission from school principal (written if possible)
- [ ] Ask teacher to brief students — "a student has built an AI study app, he's coming to show you"

**On the day:**
- [ ] Arrive 30 min early to set up, test internet
- [ ] Shoot horizontal (landscape) always
- [ ] Get at least 3 genuine reactions — you'll use the best one
- [ ] Film yourself walking in — establishes authenticity
- [ ] Get the school signboard clearly — builds credibility

**Non-negotiable shots:**
- [ ] At least one student reading a Punjabi AI answer with their face visible
- [ ] At least one student feedback clip (even 10 seconds)
- [ ] You on camera, natural, not scripted-sounding
