Explain Newton's Third Law of Motion with a real-life example.
S
Newton's Third Law states: for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

Real example: When a rocket expels gas downward (action), the gas pushes the rocket upward (reaction) — that's why rockets launch into space. 🚀

When you push against a wall, the wall pushes back on you with the same force.
📘 Physics Ch.9 Class 9 PSEB Newton's Laws
What about swimming?
S
Perfect example! In swimming, you push water backward with your arms and feet (action). The water pushes you forward (reaction). The harder you push, the faster you move.
